Puzzle Templates
Reusable formats based on your existing work
⚡ Reducing your direct labor: Each template below maps to a puzzle type you've already built. Clicking "Use Template" pre-fills the structure — you only need to swap in the member-specific content.
🪪 Employee ID Badge
PraxisOT-style badge with photo + rhyming clue
  • Company name + tagline (use client's real company)
  • Member photo (use their submitted photo)
  • Employee ID number (encode a clue in the digits)
  • 4-line rhyming clue → points to next person
"When credentials don't align / and access is denied, / Escalate to leadership— / keep the team unified."
📰 Classifieds Ad
Fake newspaper with ads hiding clues in copy
  • Fake newspaper name (use city + "Classifieds")
  • Main ad uses member's fun fact as the product/service
  • Hidden clue in ad body text (first letter of each sentence)
  • Side column ads reference other team members' snacks
Hidden acrostic: bold or italic first letters spell out the next person's name or clue word.
🍣 Fake Business Flyer
Satirical business built from member's fun fact
  • Business name derived from member's story or snack
  • Tagline is a pun or exaggeration of their fact
  • Fake "challenge" or event encodes the next clue
  • Address / phone number hides a combination code
Example: member eats 41 pieces of sushi → "SO RICE & REGRET CHALLENGE — beat the record: 41 pieces"
💌 Personal Letter
Letter from a fictional character (customer, vendor, etc.)
  • Letterhead uses client company name
  • Letter writer is a fictional fan of the company
  • Body text contains emojis that are clues (use decoder)
  • Sign-off name encodes next team member's initials
Pair with emoji decoder sheet — emojis in the letter = words that form next clue when decoded.
😀 Emoji Decoder
Emoji → word lookup sheet + hidden message
  • Create a unique emoji-to-word key per experience
  • Hidden message in the decoder itself (first letters of words)
  • Combine with the letter or ID badge as a secondary clue
  • Watch dial image = "remaining minutes in your hands"
Hint text uses italic/bold formatting to hide an acrostic: "In every report, formatting matters. Strong ideas stand bold, creative ones lean in."
📱 QR Code Redirect
QR leads to a hidden page or unlocks next clue
  • QR links to a private Google Doc, Notion page, or short URL
  • Destination page contains the next clue or team member reveal
  • "Scan me!" design adds physical interactivity to the packet
  • Works well as a final unlock or combo-lock code reveal
Tool tip: qr-code-generator.com — free, no account needed. Set redirect URL per experience.
🎨 Custom Portrait / Puzzle Image
AI-generated custom illustration from member's photo
  • Use member photo → AI cartoon/caricature (ChatGPT image gen)
  • Place in themed scene matching their fun fact
  • Wrap as physical jigsaw (puzzleprints.com ~$15/puzzle)
  • Completion image = final "CONGRATULATIONS" reveal poster
Final collage tip: compile all member stories into one illustrated scene for the congrats poster.
